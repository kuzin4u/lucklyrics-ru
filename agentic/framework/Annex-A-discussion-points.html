      CommentSituationPer the FrameworkPer our proposalProposed change
      C01Who maintains budget state
      cl. 5.5 · 6.3.2      		 Overspend discovered after the fact; the constraint was bypassed without anyone intending it. Risk: payer’s issuer. Budget respected; the second purchase awaits the consumer’s decision. Risk: no one. State held outside the Agent; atomic, single-use consumption
      C02What proves fulfilment
      cl. 5.6 · 4.3.5      		 The outcome depends on the parties’ correspondence rather than on records. Risk: unclear. Dispute resolved from the record in hours rather than weeks. Risk: merchant. Minimum fulfilment record at order level
      C03Unrecognised constraint type
      cl. 6.7.6      		 Goods shipped, no payment; participants reached different conclusions on the same mandate. Risk: merchant. A predictable decline before shipment; no loss. Risk: no one. Strict by default for payment constraints plus a shared type registry
      C04Revocation of the mandate
      cl. 5.4      		 Revoking the mandate did not stop the payment. Risk: payer’s issuer. Revocation takes effect once it is known. Risk: no one. Revocation retrievable at execution; unverifiable state treated as out of bounds
      C05Choosing the instrument at payment time
      cl. 1.2 · 1.2.1      		 Transaction fails; the mandate must be issued again. Risk: consumer. Transaction completed; the choice of instrument stays with the consumer. Risk: —. Mandate may reference an instrument class or payer-side selection point
      C06Agent execution history
      cl. 3      		 Identification does not distinguish a compliant agent from a repeatedly non-compliant one. Risk: issuer and consumer. Agent risk is reflected before the transaction rather than after a dispute. Risk: —. Execution history as a KYA element
      C07Indicator without a reference to evidence
      cl. 8      		 The evidence exists somewhere, but linking it to the transaction is hard. Risk: unclear. The dispute is resolved from references carried by the transaction itself. Risk: per the check result. Indicator carries references to Intent and fulfilment record

      Clause numbers refer to EMV® Agentic Payments – Framework for Specifications v1.0 DRAFT (1 September 2026). Comment codes C01–C07 correspond to rows 01–07 of the enclosed feedback form.

      The situations are constructed so that the difference shows up within a single transaction. The Framework as drafted does not prohibit good implementations — it does not require them; the model shows what happens when a participant chooses the simplest permissible option.
